# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

我们作为成员、贡献者和领导者，承诺让每个人都能参与我们的社区，无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残疾、种族、民族、性别、性取向、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、宗教或性取向如何。

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的准则 / Our Standards

有助于创造积极环境的行为包括：

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- 表现出对他人的同理心和善意 / Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- 尊重不同的观点和经验 / Respecting differing opinions and experiences
- 给予和优雅地接受建设性反馈 / Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 对社区整体最佳利益负责 / Accepting responsibility for our actions

不可接受的行为包括：

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- 性化语言或图像，以及任何形式的性骚扰 / Sexualized language or imagery
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬低性评论，人身攻击 / Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments
- 公开或私下的骚扰 / Harassment, public or private
- 未经明确许可，发布他人的私人信息 / Publishing others' private information
- **利用跨国、跨语言的信息差进行欺骗、割韭菜、杀猪盘** / **Using cross-border / cross-language information asymmetry to scam, deceive, or exploit**

> 特别说明：本项目连接的是"想学外语/技能"的普通人和"愿意教"的志愿者。任何把对方当流量、当韭菜的行为，一经发现立即移出社区并公示。
> Note: this project connects ordinary learners with volunteer teachers. Anyone treating others as traffic or prey will be removed and named publicly.

## 执行 / Enforcement

违规行为可报告至 `community@agentqev.uk`（域名邮箱配置中，暂用 QQ 群 864575260）。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查和处理。

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to `community@agentqev.uk`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

本项目维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、wiki 编辑、Issue 及其他贡献。

本行为准则基于 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1 版，并附加了针对跨境技能交换场景的特别条款。
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, with additional clauses for cross-border skill exchange.
