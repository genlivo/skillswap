# Vision — English (shareable)

> This is the version you can forward to anyone. It explains *what* we're building and *why* — not the business moat.

---

## One sentence

**Let people worldwide trade what they can teach for what they want to learn.**

Not a "language app" — a skill-swap marketplace. Your native tongue, your craft, is your currency.

## Why we're doing this

1. **Many in China speak only Mandarin but want to learn a foreign language** — yet on HelloTalk (70M users) they're one in millions, never matched precisely.
2. **Native Chinese speakers are scarce overseas.** "Learn Chinese" is real demand abroad, and we can supply not just language but regional culture few have seen — Zuoquan folk songs, Xiaohua opera, the Taihang Mountains.
3. **AI / Agent skills are an unclaimed category.** Global demand to learn Agent skills is exploding, and many who master it are in China. That's our moat.

## How we do it (three principles)

1. **Async-first, realtime-second.** Timezones kill scheduled calls; async + an AI companion covers every zone. The most effective language action — write → get corrected → understand — works *better* async.
2. **Open co-building, not behind closed doors.** A platform is a network-effect business; 10 highly-active users beat 10,000 zombies. We publish the vision first and let the right people walk in.
3. **Adults first, teens later.** Minor-compliance + cross-border data is the heaviest part; ship 18+ to validate, then add a teen version.

## What we share vs. keep

- **Share:** vision, roadmap, contributing guide, code of conduct, infrastructure code, doc tools, translations
- **Keep:** core matching algorithm, monetization logic, user PII, AI model weights

## Where we're going

A global, accessible peer-to-peer learning playground: Chinese kids find foreign friends to practice with; AI-curious people find teachers; anyone who wants to learn anything finds a partner.
