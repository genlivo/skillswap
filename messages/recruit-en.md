## Long version (blog / pinned post)

I'm building something from Zuoquan, China, and looking for like-minded people:

**SkillSwap · a global skill-swap playground** — not a language app, but "trade the skill you have for the skill you want."

Why:
- Many in China speak only Mandarin and want foreign friends to practice with, but on big platforms they're one in millions.
- Native Chinese speakers are scarce overseas — and we can also offer regional culture few have seen (Zuoquan folk songs, Taihang Mountains).
- AI / Agent skills are an unclaimed category, and many who master it are in China.

How we work now:
- Async-first (text + voice + correction + AI companion), no forced live video, timezone-friendly.
- Built in the open on GitHub — auditable code, real replies, no get-rich-quick schemes.
- 18+ for the MVP, free reciprocal exchange.

If you'll be among the first teachers or learners, join QQ `864575260` or email `contribute@agentqev.uk`. Site agentqev.uk is in progress.
